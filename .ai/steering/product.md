# Product Steering

> Status: Active
> Last Updated: 2026-07-31

## Product Vision

Knewzly is the working title for a history-first, interactive regional AI time atlas. It should help learners understand how artificial intelligence developed across places and periods without requiring specialist knowledge.

## Target Users / Personas

- **Curious learner:** Wants an approachable way to explore AI history and compare how regions contributed to it.
- **Guided learner:** Benefits from structured comparisons, quizzes, and saved learning trails rather than an unbounded information feed.

The experience and writing should be understandable to a 16-year-old reader.

## Value Proposition

Knewzly combines time, geography, guided comparison, and learning activities so AI history can be explored as a connected regional story rather than a flat chronology.

## Product Boundaries

### Directional Sequence

1. Curated AI history map.
2. Guided regional comparisons.
3. Learner quizzes and saved trails.
4. Automated source ingestion.
5. Present-day AI ecosystem pulse.

This sequence is planning context, not an approved implementation roadmap.

### In Scope

- Historical AI events presented through time and region.
- Guided explanations and comparisons.
- Source-aware learning content.

### Out of Scope for Initialization

- Selecting an implementation stack.
- Defining the MVP boundary.
- Treating the present-day ecosystem pulse as a first-release commitment.

## Domain Glossary

- **Time atlas:** An experience combining chronological and geographic exploration.
- **Regional comparison:** A guided view of how AI developments differed or connected across places.
- **Saved trail:** A learner-curated or system-guided path through related material.
